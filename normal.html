By Alex Rivard • April 3, 2026

"The barrier between thought and application just vanished."

I've been experimenting with the new Gemini Canvas feature lately, and the results are honestly mind-blowing. Unlike standard chat interfaces, Canvas allows the AI to generate a living, breathing workspace where it can write code, render UI, and iterate in real-time.

To test its limits, I asked Gemini to build a fully functional scientific calculator. Within 10 seconds, it produced a sleek, interactive app that handles complex equations.
